# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

## What We Process

- **Pastes:** Content you upload. **Pastes are public.** Anyone with the link can view or download them. Do not upload personal data or secrets (passwords, tokens).
- **Request Logs:** Technical data (IP address, user agent, timestamps) processed for security, rate-limiting, and abuse prevention.

## Service Providers

We use third-party hosting, storage, and CDN providers in Germany, the Netherlands, and globally, relying on GDPR-compliant safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses) for international transfers. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Retention

Pastes are automatically deleted after a limited period (currently up to 180 days for web pastes; 30 days for API pastes). Logs are kept only as necessary. We cannot recall third-party cached copies.

## Your Rights

Under UK/EU GDPR, processing is based on our legitimate interests in securing and delivering the service. You may have rights to access, delete, or object to processing. To request removal or exercise rights, [contact us](mailto:report-abuse@pastes.dev) with the paste link. We may update this policy by publishing a revised version.
